POST(P, Q)
this algorithm converts a infix arithmetic notation given in Q into a postfix notation we have to enter in P using a stack.

1.  PUSH right paranthesis to Q and Push left paranthesis to the STACK. [right paranthesis acts as a sentinel]

2.  Scan Q from left to right and repeat steps 3 to 6 until STACK becomes empty

3.  if left paranthesis is encountered, then: push it to the STACK

4.  if an operand is encountered,  push it to P

5.  if an operator is encountered, then:
    (a) pop the operators from the top of stack having equal or higher precedence than operator and push it to P
    (b) push operator to the STACK

6.  if a right paranthesis is encountered, then:
    (a) pop the elements from STACK one by one and PUSH them to the P until a left paranthesis is encountered in STACK
    (b) pop left paranthesis from the STACK
        [DO NOT ADD IT TO THE P]
[end of step 2 loop]

7.  Exit
